Privacy Policy for GeoConsent

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

At GeoConsent, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data: GeoConsent collects your location data in background to provide geo-fencing and attendance features, even when the app is closed or not in use. Location is only collected after you grant permission.

Personal Information: Name, email, phone number for account creation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use location data to mark attendance automatically when you enter assigned locations, to verify consent-based presence, and to generate reports for authorized employers/admins.

3. Background Location

Our app requires background location permission (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to function properly for automatic attendance and geo-consent verification. You can disable it anytime from system settings, but features may not work.

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. Location data is shared only with your organization admin/employer for attendance purposes, with your explicit consent given during app setup.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data anytime by contacting us. You can revoke location permission from Android Settings > Apps > GeoConsent > Permissions.
